Toward Pulse privacy policy

Last updated: September 20, 2026

Toward Pulse is a compass app published by Cory Campbell. It helps you face a city, state, or country on Earth.

What the app uses

  • Your location (GPS), while the app is open. Used on the device to know where you are standing so it can compute the direction to the place you picked. Location is requested as “When In Use” only. It is not used in the background.
  • Device heading (compass). Used on the device to know which way the top of the phone points, so the app can say Left, Right, or Facing.
  • The place you search. When you type a city, state, or country, that text is sent to Photon (a public geocoder operated independently of Toward Pulse) so the app can get coordinates for that place.
  • Last place you picked. Stored on your phone only, so the app can open to that place next time.

What the app does not do

  • No account. No sign-in.
  • No friends list, messages, or social features.
  • Does not sell your location or search text.
  • Does not build an advertising profile.
  • Does not share your GPS coordinates with Photon. Photon receives the words you type, not where you are standing.
  • Does not require the network after a place is already saved on the phone, except to search a new place.

Who can see what

Location and heading stay on your device. The last saved place stays on your device. Search text goes to Photon to resolve a place name. Cory Campbell does not operate a Toward Pulse server that stores your location.

If you later use optional over-the-air screen updates (EAS Update), the app may contact Expo to check for a new version of the app screen. That check does not include your GPS location or the place you are facing.

Turning it off

You can deny or revoke location in iPhone Settings. Toward Pulse will then show a screen that asks you to open Settings. Uninstalling the app removes the last saved place from the phone.

Children

Toward Pulse is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.
